### 2.4 V5 concept — the best realistic version

**Nutrition**
| Gap | Proposal | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Protein → 18–20 g/100 g | Pulses to 40–45 %, or 8–10 % defatted groundnut flour (~50 % protein, low fat) | Allergen; aflatoxin testing needed |
| Omega-3 | Twin-pack: main pouch + separate 5 g cold-milled flax sachet | Keeps 18-month life (oil stays out of main powder) |
| B12 / D / iodine | Separate fortified "Purna+" variant, or "best with milk" messaging | Fortification conflicts with "100 % natural" |
| Blood sugar | Dates 3 % → 1.5 %, add 0.3 % cinnamon | Less sweet |
| Iron / calcium absorption | Already good (sprouting + amla) | Keep |

**Process**
- **Malted ragi**: fully sprout and roast until it smells malty. Natural sweetness and easier digestion, so fewer dates are needed.
- **Instant-mix texture**: stir into hot milk for 10 s with no lumps, no boiling, no raw taste. How to get there:
  1. Fully pre-cook the starch in the roast (CCP-1 already mostly does this)
  2. 100-mesh, uniform particle size
  3. Malting (enzymes break down starch)
  4. Keep makhana / puffed paddy as dispersion aids (argues **against** halving them in V4.3)
  5. Later, at scale: drum-drying or extrusion for a truly instant powder
- Batch QC test: 2 spoons in hot milk, stir 10 s → no lumps, no raw taste, no grit. If it passes, the pack can say "Ready in 10 seconds — no boiling".

**Proof and trust**
- NABL full nutrition panel; print the **actual** numbers on the pack.
- Glycemic index test (rare among Indian health mixes).
- Batch QR code → that batch's lab report on livewholesome.in.

**Variants (same base)**
- Purna Kids: more malted ragi, mild cardamom, finer grind
- Purna Diabetic Care: no dates, more pulses and barley, cinnamon
- Purna Protein: defatted groundnut + pulses, ~20 g protein
- Purna Elders: finer grind, easier to digest, calcium-forward

**Claude's recommended order**

2. Trial batch of V4.3-corrected, keeping makhana/paddy at V4.2 levels (only cut cardamom)
3. Add malted ragi + the 100-mesh instant-mix process

5. Only after that: flax twin-pack, variants, GI test
